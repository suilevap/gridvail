# Figures: objects drawn as stacks of glyph parts by renderers that show
# height (the text renderer's `--walls stacked` and `--walls leaning`), in
# place of their glyph. Flat renderers draw the glyph as before.
#
# A figure is drawn as seen from above, facing north (up the screen), one
# part a line, bottom to top. A part is a glyph lying flat, its ink standing
# from one height to another (in cells above the floor; walls stand 0.625),
# like a slice of the figure: what stands higher is seen on top, what stands
# lower shows below it toward the viewer. The whole figure turns to the way
# the object faces.
#
#   LABEL GLYPH FROM TO [options]
#
# LABEL names the part for the reader. GLYPH is one character, or `own` for
# the object's own glyph. Options:
#   x=X y=Y      where the glyph's cell is centred, in cells from the
#                object's centre (x to the right, y toward the back: -y is
#                ahead)
#   scale=S      the glyph's size, 1 for a whole cell
#   shade=S      how much of the light it shows, 0 to 1 (default 1)
#   tint=#rrggbb a colour the light is multiplied by, instead of a shade
#   upright      the glyph does not turn with the figure (its place does)
#   only=GLYPHS  drawn only while the object's glyph is one of these
