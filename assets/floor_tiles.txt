# Floor tiles for the floor shader (`--floor NAME`, V in game).
#
# A glyph is an 8 by 8 picture: `glyph NAME`, then eight rows of `#` (on)
# and `.` (off). The first few are Unscii's own, as the game's font draws
# them; the rest are made for floors.
#
# A style lays glyphs out on the floor, each lit as a whole by the light
# at its middle:
#
#   style NAME slots=N [shift=S] [jitter=J] [empty=E] [gain=G]
#         [block=WxH | ramp] pick=GLYPH,GLYPH,...
#
#   slots   glyphs along a cell's side: 1, 2 or 4; a glyph fills its slot
#   shift   how many slots every other row is moved along (bricks)
#   jitter  how many of its pixels a glyph may stray, at random
#   empty   the share of slots left empty, at random
#   gain    how bright lit glyphs are (1.7 if not given)
#   pick    glyphs chosen at random (the same at the same place), or, with
#           block=WxH, laid out as a block W slots wide and H high, row by
#           row, repeated across the floor so neighbours join up, or, with
#           ramp, chosen by how bright it is there, darkest first
